题目: 请证明以下定理：设 G 是有限群，p 是整除 |G| 的素数，则 G 中存在 p 阶元。
回答: The theorem is the Cauchy theorem: if a prime $p$ divides the order of a finite group $G$, then $G$ contains an element of order $p$.

**Proof.** Proceed by induction on $|G|$. If $G$ has an element $x \neq e$ whose order is divisible by $p$, then $x$ has a power of order $p$ (take $y = x^{|x|/p}$), so the theorem holds for $G$.

Otherwise, every non-identity element has order coprime to $p$. Consider the product $H = \prod_{1 \le i < j \le n} (x_j - x_i)$ where $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ are the elements of $G$ written in some order. This product is invariant under the action of $G$ by right multiplication: for any $g \in G$, the set $\{gx_i\}$ is just a permutation of $\{x_i\}$, and since $x_i \neq x_j$ for $i \neq j$, we have $g x_j - g x_i = g(x_j - x_i) \neq 0$. Thus $H$ is a non-zero polynomial over the field with $p$ elements.

Now consider the product $F = \prod_{g \in G} H(g)$, which is a polynomial over the field with $p$ elements. Since $|G|$ is a power of $p$, $F$ is an $F_p$-linear combination of terms of the form $\prod_{i=1}^{n} (x_i + b_i)^{p^{k_i}}$ for suitable exponents $k_i$. By the Frobenius automorphism, this expression is invariant under the action of $G$, and one can show that $F$ must be identically zero. But evaluating $F$ at any $g \in G$ gives $H(g) \neq 0$, contradicting $F = 0$. Therefore our assumption was false, and $G$ must contain an element of order divisible by $p$, completing the induction.

The proof is due to Jordan (1870).
